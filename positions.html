Open Positions

We are looking for passionate scientists to join our team. We welcome applications from candidates with diverse backgrounds and experiences.

Join the Alon Lab

Our lab combines structural biology, pharmacology, biochemistry, cell biology, and computational methods to study sterol synthesis, recognition, and signaling. We are committed to fostering an inclusive, collaborative, and rigorous scientific environment.

Postdoctoral Fellow

Accepting Applications

We are seeking highly motivated postdoctoral fellows with a strong background in structural biology, biochemistry, cell biology, or a related field. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to lead independent research projects aimed at understanding the molecular mechanisms of sterol sensing and signaling.

Requirements

  • PhD in biochemistry, biophysics, structural biology, cell biology, pharmacology, or a related field
  • Experience with one or more of: cryo-EM, X-ray crystallography, protein biochemistry, cell-based assays, or computational methods
  • Strong publication record demonstrating scientific productivity
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary and benefits following NIH pay scale
  • Access to state-of-the-art cryo-EM and X-ray facilities at Yale
  • Mentorship focused on career development
  • Vibrant scientific community at Yale School of Medicine
  • Opportunity to publish in high-impact journals

How to Apply

Please send a single PDF containing (1) a cover letter describing your research interests and career goals, (2) a CV including a list of publications, and (3) contact information for three references to:

assaf.alon@yale.edu

Graduate Student

Accepting Rotations

We welcome graduate students from Yale's PhD programs, including the Pharmacology, Biochemistry & Biophysics, and Cell Biology programs. Rotation students are encouraged to reach out to learn about ongoing projects and arrange a visit.

What You Will Learn

  • Structural biology techniques including cryo-EM and X-ray crystallography
  • Protein expression, purification, and biochemical characterization
  • Cell-based assays for membrane protein function
  • Computational methods for structure prediction and analysis
  • Scientific writing and presentation skills

Interested?

Current Yale graduate students interested in rotating should reach out directly. Please include a brief description of your research background and interests.

assaf.alon@yale.edu

Undergraduate Researcher

Accepting Applications

We are enthusiastic about training the next generation of scientists. Yale undergraduates interested in gaining research experience in structural biology and biochemistry are encouraged to apply. We offer both semester-long and summer research positions.

Requirements

  • Currently enrolled Yale undergraduate student
  • Strong interest in biochemistry, structural biology, or related fields
  • Ability to commit a minimum of 10 hours per week during the semester
  • Prior lab experience is a plus but not required

🎓 Academic Year

Part-time research for course credit (CHEM or MCDB independent study). Minimum 10 hrs/week commitment.

☀️ Summer Research

Full-time summer research positions available. Funding through Yale STARS, SURF, or similar programs encouraged.

How to Apply

Send an email with your CV/resume and a brief paragraph describing your interest in the lab and your availability.

assaf.alon@yale.edu

Visiting Scientist / Scholar

Inquire for Availability

We occasionally host visiting scientists, scholars, and high school students for short-term research experiences. If you are interested in visiting the lab, please reach out with a description of your background, research interests, and proposed timeline.

Who Should Apply

  • Scientists on sabbatical looking to learn new techniques
  • International students seeking short-term research exchange
  • Advanced high school students with demonstrated interest in science
  • Industry scientists interested in academic collaboration

Get in Touch

Please send an email describing your background, the type of visit you are interested in, and your proposed dates of stay.

assaf.alon@yale.edu
