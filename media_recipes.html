Miniprep Buffer

P1 — Resuspension Buffer

  • 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0
  • 10 mM EDTA
  • 100 µg/ml RNase A
  • 43 µg/ml Thymophthalein (blue pH indicator)

⚠️ Keep refrigerated. Shake well before using.

1000X Blue pH indicator dye: 43 mg/ml Thymophthalein in 100% ethanol

Miniprep Buffer

P2 — Lysis Buffer

  • 200 mM NaOH
  • 1% SDS
Miniprep Buffer

N3 — Neutralization Buffer

For spin columns

  • 4.2 M guanidine hydrochloride (GuHCl)
  • 0.9 M potassium acetate pH 4.8
Miniprep Buffer

P3 — Neutralization Buffer

For bacmid / midi / maxi / giga preps

  • 3.0 M potassium acetate pH 5.5
⚠️ Do not use with spin columns!
Miniprep Buffer

PB — PCR Cleanup / EndA+ Removal

Extra wash step for EndA+ removal or PCR purification

  • 5 M guanidine hydrochloride (Gu-HCL)
  • 30% isopropanol
Miniprep Buffer

PE — Wash Buffer

  • 80% ethanol
  • 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5 (25°C)
Miniprep Buffer

QG — Gel Extraction Buffer

  • 5.5 M guanidine thiocyanate (GuSCN)
  • 20 mM Tris-HCl, pH 6.6 (25°C)
Growth Media

Terrific Broth (TB)

For 1 L

  • 47.6 g TB mix
  • 4 ml glycerol
  • Add water to 1 L
  • Autoclave at 121°C for 15–20 minutes

After autoclave add:

  • 0.1%–1% glucose
  • 10 mM MgSO₄
  • Antibiotics
Growth Media

Super Optimal Broth (SOB)

For 1 L

  • 20 g Tryptone
  • 5 g Yeast Extract
  • 0.5 g NaCl (~8.5 mM)
  • 0.186 g KCl (2.5 mM)
  • Add water to 1 L
  • Autoclave at 121°C for 15–20 minutes

After autoclave add:

  • 5 ml of sterile 2 M MgCl2
Growth Media

YPAD

For 1 L

  • 10 g Yeast extract
  • 20 g Peptone
  • 80 mg Adenine sulfate
  • 20 g Agar (for plates only)
  • Dissolve in 950 ml water and autoclave

After autoclave add:

  • 20 g Dextrose (Glucose) dissolved in 50 ml water and filter sterilized
Growth Media

Yeast Drop-out Media

For 1 L

  • 1.92 g drop-out mix
  • 6.7 g yeast nitrogen base w/o AA
  • 20 g carbohydrate (glucose/galactose)

For plates add:

  • 20 g Agar
Other Buffers

20X HEPES Buffered Saline (HBS)

  • 400 mM HEPES pH 7.5
  • 3 M NaCl
